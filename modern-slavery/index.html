Modern slavery statement

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Poho Ltd is below the turnover threshold that requires a statement under section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015. We publish this statement voluntarily, because we believe every business should be clear about where it stands.

Our business

Poho Ltd is a small Edinburgh-based company providing technology leadership and software for the vehicle salvage, dismantling and recycling industry. Our work is professional and knowledge-based, and our supply chain is limited, mainly to established technology, software and professional service providers.

Our commitment

We do not tolerate slavery, servitude, forced or compulsory labour, or human trafficking, in our own business or in our supply chain.

What we do

  • We work with reputable, established suppliers and partners
  • We expect those we work with to meet the same standards and to comply with the Modern Slavery Act 2015
  • We will not work with any supplier where we have reason to believe these standards are not met
  • We review this statement each year and as our business grows

Raising a concern

If you have a concern about modern slavery connected with Poho Ltd, please contact us in confidence at info@poho.uk.

This statement is approved by the director of Poho Ltd.

Dave Harcourt, Director
