Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how Poho Ltd ("we", "us") handles personal information in connection with this website and the applications we operate.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply to you.

Google account access

Poho Ltd operates an internal application that uses Google APIs solely to store and restore its own system backups in its own Google Drive. It is not available to the public.

  • It accesses only the files it creates in the signed-in user's Google Drive
  • Data obtained through Google APIs is used solely to store and restore those backups
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google Account's security settings

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete any personal information we hold about you. Contact us at privacy@poho.uk.
