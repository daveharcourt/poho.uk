Anti-bribery policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Poho Ltd has zero tolerance of bribery and corruption. We conduct our business honestly and in line with the Bribery Act 2010, wherever we work and whoever we work with.

What this means in practice

  • We never offer, promise, give, request or accept a bribe, in any form
  • We do not make facilitation payments to speed up routine processes
  • Gifts and hospitality must be modest, infrequent and openly given, and never intended to influence a decision
  • We do not make political donations on behalf of the company
  • We expect the same standards from anyone acting on our behalf, including subcontractors and partners

Records

We keep accurate records of payments, gifts and hospitality given or received, and of our dealings with third parties.

Raising a concern

If you have a concern about bribery or corruption connected with Poho Ltd, please contact us in confidence at info@poho.uk. Anyone raising a concern in good faith will not suffer any detriment for doing so.

Responsibility

This policy is approved by the director of Poho Ltd and reviewed each year.
